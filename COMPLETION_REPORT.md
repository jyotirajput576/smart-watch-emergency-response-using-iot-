## 📊 Features Summary

### Alert Detection Monitors

```
❤️ Heart Rate Analysis
├── Normal: 60-100 BPM
├── Warning: 100-120 BPM
└── Critical: >120 BPM

🩸 Blood Pressure Analysis
├── Normal: <120/80
├── Warning: 130-140 / 80-90
└── Critical: >180/120

🫁 Oxygen Saturation
├── Normal: >95%
├── Warning: 92-95%
└── Critical: <92%

🌡️ Temperature
├── Normal: 36.5-37.5°C
├── Warning: 38-39°C
└── Critical: >39°C

🍬 Blood Sugar (Diabetic)
├── Safe: 70-180 mg/dL
├── Warning: 180-300
└── Critical: <70 or >300

😰 Stress & Anxiety
├── Low Stress + Normal HR
├── High Stress + Elevated HR
└── Critical: HR >120 + High Stress
```
